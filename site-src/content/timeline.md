## Risks already identified

| Risk | Why it matters | Early mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Grant cycle missed | The FY26 first step has passed | Prepare the next cycle; check whether the county already filed; pursue parallel sources |
| Aircraft or payload not eligible | Blue status is configuration-specific and changes | Save dated DCMA evidence; get the awarding agency's written approval before purchase |
| Package exceeds $200,000 | Camera, link, site, and training can exceed the aircraft cost | Capped line items; host-funded private elements; a reduced-scope alternate |
| Aircraft weight crosses 55 lb | Changes the FAA path | Weigh the complete configuration; prefer a sub-55 configuration for Part 107 |
| Remote link fails | Loss of control or video is a safety risk | Dual backhaul, local failsafes, tested lost-link routes, UPS, health monitoring |
| Altitude, heat, and wind cut endurance | Brochure tests may be near sea level | Require local payload demonstration and conservative reserves |
| Hybrid fuel, noise, and maintenance burden | Launch-ready storage is harder than battery docking | Code-compliant enclosure, scheduled inspections, trained servicing, a noise test |
| Public concern over surveillance | Can stop the project or damage trust | Publish policy early; no facial recognition; limited retention; audit logs; reporting |
| Host or customer feed exposes evidence | Privacy and case risk | Separate streams and accounts; county kill switch; delayed or sanitized view |
| Plate-reading expectations exceed optics | Performance varies sharply | Controlled testing; publish measured results only |
| Overlap with existing city and county programs | Duplication weakens the grant | Treat interoperability and regional coverage as design requirements |
| Vendor lock-in | Proprietary controls and subscriptions create recurring cost | Require data export, documented interfaces, support terms, an exit plan |

Sources: the research report, sections 2, 18, and 19 · <a href="https://pueblo-drone-pilot.stoagen.com/report.md">report.md</a> · checked 2026-08-23
