# Sources and downloads

## The research report

The full research report behind this site, dated August 23, 2026, is published at [report.md](../report.md) (also served as [report.md.txt](../report.md.txt)). It carries every source link beside its claims, the aircraft appendix, the grant analysis, and a machine-readable summary. The prospective host business and its owner are unnamed in the published copy by design. [[verified]]

## How to read the claim states

Everything on this site carries one of four states, shown as a labeled chip: [[verified]] a fact with a source, checked on the stated date; [[estimate]] a planning figure, labeled with where it came from; [[proposal]] something suggested and not yet decided by anyone; [[open]] a question nobody has answered yet. A time-sensitive status (a Blue-list entry, a grant deadline, a price) is only as good as its check date and must be reverified when it matters.
