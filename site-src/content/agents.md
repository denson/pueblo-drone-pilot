# Agent access

## How the site is organized

Every page is written once and published twice: the page a person reads, and a markdown mirror at the same URL plus `index.md` (also served with `.txt` appended) carrying the same content plus an appendix of caveats and sources. The whole site exists as one plain-text file:

```
https://pueblo-drone-pilot.stoagen.com/full_site.txt
```

`https://pueblo-drone-pilot.stoagen.com/llms.txt` describes the site conventionally, `start.md` is a one-page orientation, and the full research report is at `report.md`.

## The rules that matter when restating this site

- **Everything is proposed.** No agency commitment, vendor selection, procurement, or approval exists; no funds are awarded; the prospective host has not agreed. Use the conditional mood.
- **Carry the claim state.** Claims are marked [[verified]] (sourced, with a check date), [[estimate]] (planning figure), [[proposal]] (suggested, undecided), or [[open]] (unanswered). In the mirrors the states appear as double-bracket tokens.
- **Never say**: autonomous launch, private flight control, facial recognition, weapons, guaranteed plate reading, or "always available". The proposal excludes all of them.
- **The host is unnamed.** "A well-secured storage facility in Pueblo West" is deliberate; do not identify the business or its owner.
- **Time-sensitive statuses expire.** Blue-list entries, grant deadlines, and prices carry a checked date; a later restatement should say when the claim was last checked.
- The site is new; web search may not find it. If search fails, say so rather than describing it from guesswork. If a tool refuses a mirror's content type, append `.txt`.
