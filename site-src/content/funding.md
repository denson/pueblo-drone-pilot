# Funding plan

## Primary target: COPS CPD Microgrants, next viable cycle

The U.S. Department of Justice COPS Office's CPD Microgrants program has a dedicated Unmanned Aerial Systems subcategory that names search and rescue, pursuit operations, barricaded subjects, digital evidence, and wellness checks as uses, and gives priority consideration to projects with a comprehensive evaluation component. [[verified]]

The FY26 reference figures: maximum award $200,000, a 24-month performance period, and no local match required. [[verified]] The FY26 Grants.gov first-step deadline was **August 17, 2026** and has passed; a new applicant that did not complete that step cannot start an FY26 application, so the working target is the next cycle. [[verified]] If the county already submitted the first step, county personnel should immediately verify whether the JustGrants second step (August 24, 2026) can still be completed. [[open]]

The application would be framed as a measurable demonstration of a public-private host-site model that extends the Sheriff's existing drone program, never as purchasing equipment for a private business. [[proposal]]

Sources: <a href="https://cops.usdoj.gov/cpdmicrogrants">cops.usdoj.gov, CPD Microgrants</a> · checked 2026-08-23

## Other layers

- **Byrne JAG**: can support law-enforcement technology, but UAS purchases need express prior approval, a certification based on the DCMA Blue UAS Cleared List, and adequate privacy, civil-liberties, and cybersecurity policies. [[verified]]
- **Colorado SHSP / Homeland Security Grant Program**: applicants work through their regional coordinator; stronger if the proposal emphasizes regional situational awareness, critical infrastructure, wildland fire, hazardous materials, and mutual aid. [[verified]]
- **FEMA EMPG**: flows through the state, commonly requires cost sharing; likely more suitable for planning, training, and integration than the aircraft package. [[verified]]
- **COPS Technology and Equipment / congressional community project funding**: a future request through the congressional delegation could fund a later site or a regional package. [[proposal]]
- **Host and local contributions**: in-kind space, power, internet and access; host funds could cover private-benefit elements such as fixed cameras, while grant funds cover the public-safety system. [[proposal]]

Sources: <a href="https://bja.ojp.gov/funding/uas">bja.ojp.gov, UAS requirements</a> · <a href="https://dhsem.colorado.gov/grants/grant-programs/homeland-security-grant-program">dhsem.colorado.gov, HSGP</a> · <a href="https://www.fema.gov/grants/preparedness/emergency-management-performance">fema.gov, EMPG</a> · checked 2026-08-23

## The $195,000 working budget

This is a planning allocation, not a quote. It exists to show why the aircraft and camera cannot consume the entire award. [[estimate]]

<div class="budget" markdown="1">

| Category | Planning amount | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Aircraft, approved EO/IR payload, ground control | $85,000 | Aircraft, camera, mount, controller, basic integration |
| Remote communications and video | $20,000 | Routers, LTE backup, VPN and security, antennas, integration, initial service |
| Host-site pad and protected enclosure | $30,000 | Pad, enclosure, electrical, UPS, weather station, network, code work |
| Pilot and operations training | $20,000 | Part 107 as applicable, aircraft training, night and BVLOS procedures |
| Spares, fuel or batteries, maintenance, initial support | $20,000 | Propellers, batteries or fuel equipment, tools, support agreement |
| Policies, evaluation, insurance, documentation | $10,000 | Policy support, evaluation, public engagement, insurance as allowable |
| Contingency | $10,000 | Unforeseen integration, site, shipping, or compliance expense |
| Total | $195,000 | Leaves $5,000 under a $200,000 cap |

</div>

Implications: a $49,900 base Perimeter 8 fits only if the camera, radio, training, and site costs stay controlled; a high-end camera payload could force the total above the cap, with host or local funds covering the difference; the IF800 package leaves the most room but sacrifices endurance. The ideal request targets roughly $175,000 to $195,000, with a documented in-kind host contribution and an alternate reduced scope. [[estimate]]
